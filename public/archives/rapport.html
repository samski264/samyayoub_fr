Rapport d'expérience

GENERATIVE

This report documents an experiment carried out over the year. I was fascinated by AI-based creation, so I chose to work with GANs. The goal was to create artworks in order to explore what artificial creativity could look like.

I first went through an initial experimentation phase using human faces, then moved on to creating unique pieces largely inspired by contemporary art.
