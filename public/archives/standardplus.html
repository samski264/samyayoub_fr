standard+ is a platform dedicated to produce design home objects like lamp and small storage on demand with an highly automated 3D printed process and an NFC/blockchain certification.

Learn more ↓

→ Problems :

90% of French consumers often perceive local design as too expensive compared to its perceived value.


85% of designers and creators only receive 1–3% of the revenues from their creations.

→ Solution :

Make design accessible through technology. Revolutionizing the distribution of design objects through 3D printing and a digital library of objects.

→ For on-demand, certified, local, and recyclable everyday objects.

How it works :

  • Designers and creators upload their digital objects to the platform…

  • Customers explore and easily purchase 3D-printed design objects…

  • They benefit from copyright protection while accessing efficient, traceable, and scalable 3D manufacturing…

  • Produced locally, empowering independent designers and making design more affordable and accessible.

→ Tech stack :

I developed the entire MVP from scratch using modern web technologies. The goal was to make a fully automated system — from digital order to physical 3D print.


I built :

  • From-scratch marketplace: in Next.js with an express.js API and a MongoDB database. Checkout flow with custom stripe and NextAuth integration.

  • AI and data: vector-embedding-based object library exploration and OpenAI API integration.

  • Certification and security: cryptographic NFC chip that contains off-chain data to certificate object.

  • Infrastructure: AWS EC2 hosting the express API and a dockerized 3D-slicing service. Google Cloud Storage used for server-side Google Tag Manager and analytics.

→ Design system :

After several iterations, I designed a visual system that lets the objects take center stage. The goal was to build a minimalist and modular identity where the interface disappears — letting each 3D piece express its own materiality, light, and form. This system defines consistent rules for typography, color, and layout to harmonize both the digital platform and physical packaging.

→ Marketing :

To grow the platform, I designed and managed several digital campaigns on Meta Ads — combining my 3D and motion design skills to create high-performing visuals. Each campaign was optimized with data-driven iterations, reaching a 4–5% conversion rate and generating hundreds of qualified leads at a very low CAC. Beyond visuals, I also built the full marketing pipeline: audience targeting, tracking (GTM / GA4 server-side), and creative testing.
